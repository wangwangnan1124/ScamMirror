# Privacy

ScamMirror v0.1.0 is designed to minimize collection by default.

## What the product processes

The text you paste is analyzed by deterministic rules in your browser. The product does not send that text to a model API, application database, or analytics service.

The interface keeps the current text and result only in page memory. Refreshing or closing the page clears that state.

## What the product does not do

- It does not require an account.
- It does not upload screenshots.
- It does not store pasted messages.
- It does not automatically open links or QR codes found in the input.
- It does not sell or use input content for advertising or model training.

## Hosting metadata

As with most websites, the hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, timestamp, browser type, and requested path for security and operations. The pasted message is not included in those web requests by ScamMirror v0.1.0.

## External links

The footer links to official reference pages. Those sites receive a request only after you choose to open the link and are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Future AI or OCR features

Any future feature that sends text or images away from the device must be optional, visibly disclosed before processing, documented here, and protected by a defined retention policy. It must not silently reuse the local-analysis consent.

## Safe contributions

Do not submit real chat logs, phone numbers, bank details, identity documents, verification codes, or screenshots without explicit authorization and effective de-identification.
